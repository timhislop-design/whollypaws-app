How It Works

Four steps. Safe first,
savings always second.

WhollyPaws runs your pet's profile through a constraint engine before a single price is compared. Anything that fails your pet's profile is removed entirely — not deprioritized. Gone.

01

Set up each pet

Species, breed, age, weight, health conditions, and any ingredient restrictions or vet-prescribed diet flags. This is the constraint engine's input.

02

We filter first

Every product across every store is checked against your pet's profile. Unsafe products are removed before any price comparison runs. Not ranked lower — removed.

03

Cross-store pricing

What passes is compared across Chewy, PetSmart, Petco, and Tractor Supply — by unit weight, not bag size. Honest apples-to-apples comparison.

04

Your savings list

One list, organized by store, with net Found Money shown after delivery fees or gas costs. The number in your pocket — never the flattering gross.

What WhollyPaws Does

Built for how pets
actually eat and live.

🐾

Multi-pet household support

Manage dogs, cats, rabbits, and more in one household. Each pet has its own profile and constraint set. Mixed-pet households work exactly as you'd expect.

🌾

Ingredient constraint engine

Grain-free, chicken-free, fish-free, beef-free, corn/wheat/soy-free, single-protein, limited ingredient. Every flag is a hard rule that removes products — not a filter preference.

📋

Vet diet & prescription flags

Log prescription diet requirements directly. WhollyPaws surfaces appropriate options and flags anything that conflicts with vet-prescribed nutrition targets.

📦

Unit weight comparison

Prices compared per pound or per ounce — not per bag. A 30lb bag vs. a 12lb bag vs. a 40lb bag all reduce to the same unit cost so you're comparing honestly.

🚚

Delivery vs. in-store math

Chewy Autoship savings, PetSmart Repeat Delivery discounts, and in-store gas costs are all factored in. You see the real number — not the number that flatters the recommendation.

📈

Found Money ledger

Every week's savings tracked over time. See your cumulative Found Money, your best weeks, and how much the constraint engine has saved you by keeping unsafe products off your list.

Safe First — Always

The constraint engine
runs before the optimizer.

Most pet food comparison tools show you price, then let you filter. WhollyPaws does it the other way around — constraints run first, prices second. A cheaper bag that's wrong for your pet isn't savings. It's a vet bill.

Breed-specific constraints

Large-breed calcium ratios, Westie skin sensitivities, Bulldog breathing-safe formulas, and hundreds of breed-specific flags built in and updateable.

Life stage matching

Puppy, adult, senior. Kitten, adult cat. Each has different nutritional requirements — WhollyPaws enforces them, not just suggests them.

Ingredient rejection logging

Every product removed is logged with the reason. You see exactly what was filtered and why — radical transparency from the Sincere Strategy®.

The Sincere Strategy®

No pet food brand can pay
to appear in your plan.
Not one. Ever.

The pet food industry is a $50B+ market built largely on sponsored recommendations, affiliate commissions, and paid placements. WhollyPaws is funded exclusively by the households it serves — which means our only incentive is to find what's genuinely best for your pet at the best honest price.

🚫

Zero paid placements. Ever.

No brand can pay to appear in a WhollyPaws recommendation, comparison, or plan — at any price. This is a founding operating constraint, not a policy we can revisit.

🛡️

Safety is a hard rule, not a filter.

Ingredient constraints, breed restrictions, and vet diet flags are absolute. They run before the price optimizer. A cheaper bag that's wrong for your pet is never shown.

👁️

Radical transparency.

Every product removed by the constraint engine is logged and shown to you, with the exact reason. No black boxes. You see exactly what we filtered and why.

🔒
