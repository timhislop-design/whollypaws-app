The Constraint Engine

Pets have allergens too.
We take them seriously.

WhollyPaws runs pet-specific constraints before the price optimizer — just like WhollyFare does for human households. What's unsafe never reaches your list, at any price.

Species & Breed

Dog vs. cat vs. small animal. Breed-appropriate formulas. Size and weight class. Large-breed puppy calcium ratios. Breed sensitivities — Westies, Bulldogs, and others — handled as hard constraints.

Ingredient Filters

Grain-free, chicken-free, fish-free, beef-free. No corn, wheat, or soy. Single-protein. Limited ingredient. All run as hard rules — never traded for a better price. A cheaper bag that harms your pet isn't savings.

Life Stage

Puppy, adult, senior. Kitten, adult cat, senior cat. Prescription diet flags. Dental health formulas. The right food for where your pet is in life — not just the cheapest option in the aisle.

How It Works

Safe first.

Set up your pet

Species, breed, age, weight, and any ingredient sensitivities or vet-prescribed restrictions. Your pet's profile is the constraint engine's input.
