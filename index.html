How It Works

Your pet's health profile.
Then the price comparison.

01
Build your pet profile
Species, breed, age, weight, dietary restrictions, vet-prescribed diets. As many pets as your household has. Each profile is independent.
02
Health filter runs first
Before any price is shown, every product is checked against each pet's constraints. Failing products are removed — not ranked lower. Removed.
03
See the real comparison
Only safe products appear. Prices pulled from PetSmart, Chewy, Walmart, Target, Petco, and your local stores. Unit cost per pound or per serving.
04
Net savings, honestly
Delivery costs and gas are in the math. If ordering online saves $8 but shipping costs $10, we say so. You get the number that matters.

Safety First

Your pet can't read the label.
We can.

WhollyPaws runs every ingredient list through your pet's health profile before any price comparison happens. This is not a preference filter. It is a constraint engine. A product that fails is not shown — at any price.

🌾
Grain-free diets
Corn, wheat, soy, barley — tracked by ingredient, not just label claim. "Grain-free" on the bag doesn't mean grain-free in the formula.
🐔
Protein exclusions
Chicken, beef, lamb, fish — individual protein allergies tracked per pet. Multi-pet households with different restrictions handled independently.
💊
Vet-prescribed diets
Renal, hepatic, urinary, cardiac — prescription diet requirements are hard constraints. Your vet's instructions are in the filter, not in a note field.
📋
Full rejection log
Every product that didn't appear in your results has a reason. You can see it. Radical transparency is not optional — it's structural.
